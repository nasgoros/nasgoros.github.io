Installation

General steps for merlinx. Installing a custom ROM wipes your data — back up first.

  1. Unlock the bootloader

    Use the official Mi Unlock tool. Xiaomi may require a waiting period.

  2. Update firmware

    Install the stock MIUI firmware version required by LineageOS for merlinx.

  3. Flash the recovery

    Reboot to fastboot (Power + Volume Down) and flash the recovery image from the release.

    fastboot flash recovery NasgorOS-Recovery-17.0-<date>.img
  4. Format data

    Boot into recovery (Power + Volume Up) and choose Factory reset → Format data.

  5. Sideload nasgorOS

    Choose Apply update → Apply from ADB, then on your computer:

    adb sideload NasgorOS-17.0-<date>-UNOFFICIAL-merlinx.zip
  6. Optional: flash LiteGapps

    Still in recovery, sideload LiteGapps before the first boot if you want Google apps.

  7. Reboot

    The first boot can take several minutes.

nasgorOS is unofficial software provided as-is. You are responsible for your device; flashing may void your warranty.

repo sync -c -j8 --no-clone-bundle --no-tags
source build/envsetup.sh
